About us

Geonix Software is a small team. We don't have a sales department, and most of what we build starts with someone telling us about a specific, annoying problem at their shop.

Geonix Wrench came out of a conversation with a shop owner who was spending almost an hour every evening writing up job cards from memory. He wanted to just talk through what he did on each car and have someone else write it down. So we built that.

We keep the list of things we build short on purpose. Before we sell anything, we use it ourselves and fix what's annoying about it first.

How we work

  • We talk to the people who'll actually use what we build before we write any code.
  • We ship one thing at a time and fix what's broken before adding more.
  • If a feature doesn't get used, we take it out.
