A small team, a short list

We don't have a sales department. Most of what we build starts with someone telling us about a specific, annoying problem at their shop.

Where Wrench came from

A shop owner was spending almost an hour every evening writing up job cards from memory. He wanted to talk through what he'd done on each car and have someone else write it down. So we built that.

How we work

We talk to the people who'll use a thing before writing any code, ship one piece at a time, and fix what's broken before adding more. If a feature doesn't get used, we take it out.
