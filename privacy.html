Privacy Policy

Geonix Wrench records what a mechanic says about a job and turns it into a job card. This page says what that means for your data in plain terms.

Last updated 17 August 2026.

Who is responsible

Geonix Software is the data controller for Geonix Wrench. Contact: geonix.software@proton.me.

What we collect

  • Your account. Email address, and a user name you choose so colleagues can invite you to a shop. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase; we never see or store your password.
  • Your recordings, briefly. Audio you record is uploaded, transcribed, and then deleted from the server. It is not retained after the job card is produced.
  • Your job cards. The vehicle, the work performed, labour time, parts, prices, and the transcript the job card was extracted from. Transcripts are kept because they are the record of what was actually said, and mechanics rely on being able to check the card against it.
  • Your shop. Shop name, seat count, members, invitations, and your uploaded shop logo.
  • Billing. Handled by Stripe. We store your subscription status, plan, seat count, renewal date and Stripe identifiers. We never see or store your card details — those go to Stripe directly.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Under the GDPR, we rely on performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) for everything needed to deliver the product you paid for — your account, job cards, shop and subscription. Where we rely on a legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) it is limited to keeping the service secure and working, such as rate limiting to prevent abuse.

Who else processes it

These are our sub-processors. Each acts on our instructions:

  • Google Firebase — authentication (your email and login identity).
  • Stripe — payments and subscription management.
  • Anthropic — extracting structured job card fields from your transcript. Where the shop runs a self-hosted model instead, no transcript leaves that server.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

  • Audio recordings — deleted as soon as the job card is generated.
  • Job cards and transcripts — until you delete them or close your account.
  • Account and shop data — until you close your account.
  • Billing records — retained by Stripe as long as tax and accounting law requires, independently of your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This erases your account, your job cards and their transcripts, and cancels any active subscription. It cannot be undone.

If you own a shop with other mechanics in it, remove them or hand the shop over first — otherwise deleting your account would take away their workplace and their access to the shop's job cards. Job cards belonging to a shop stay with the shop.

You can also email geonix.software@proton.me and we will do it for you.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to how it is used, and receive a copy in a portable format. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Geonix Wrench is a tool for working repair shops and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Job card PDFs that the app keeps as its own archive copy are encrypted at rest on your device. No system is perfect; if you find a problem, please tell us at geonix.software@proton.me.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for anything that materially affects you, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
