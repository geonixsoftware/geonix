The problem

Written up while the job is still fresh

Most job cards get filled in from memory at the end of a long day. A part here, an extra half hour there — over a month that's real money you earned and never billed.

Voice in, job card out

Describe the vehicle, the work and the parts out loud. That's the whole input.

Nothing quietly guessed

An unclear model, quantity or part name gets flagged for you — never invented.

Catches what gets missed

Parts mentioned in passing but never itemised are called out before you invoice.

How it works

Three steps, start to finish

Record

Describe the job like you're telling a coworker — the vehicle, what you did, how long it took, which parts you used.

Review

The recording comes back as a job card. Anything Wrench wasn't sure of is marked so you can correct it in a tap.

Generate

Export the PDF with your shop logo on it. Ready to print, hand over, or attach to an invoice.
