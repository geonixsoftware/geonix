Info

Most job cards get written up after the fact, from memory, at the end of a long day. Details get left out — a part here, an extra half hour there. Geonix Wrench is built to catch that while the job is still fresh, without making you type anything.

Voice in, job card out

Describe the vehicle, the work, and the parts out loud. That's the whole input.

Nothing quietly guessed

If something's unclear — a model, a quantity, a part name — it gets flagged instead of made up.

Catches what gets forgotten

Parts or labor mentioned in passing but never itemized are called out before you invoice.

A real PDF at the end

Once it looks right, generate a job card you can hand to the front desk or attach to an invoice.

How it works

1

Record

Tap record and describe the job like you're telling a coworker. Mention the vehicle, what you did, how long it took, and any parts you used.

2

Review

The recording gets turned into a job card. Anything unclear — a mumbled part number, a vehicle model that didn't come through — gets flagged so you can check it, not guessed at silently.

3

Generate

Once it looks right, generate the PDF. It's ready to print, hand off, or attach to an invoice.
